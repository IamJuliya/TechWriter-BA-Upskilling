## Document Index

The following documents present the Business Analysis activities and deliverables in a logical sequence.

| No. | Document | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | 	[Business Case](01.Business%20Case.md) | Establishes the business need, justification, expected benefits, and financial assessment. |
| 02 | [Business Requirements Document (BRD)](02_Business_Requirements_Document.md) | Defines business objectives, scope, stakeholders, business requirements, business rules, assumptions, and constraints. |
| 03 | [User Stories and Acceptance Criteria](03_User_Stories_and_Acceptance_Criteria.md) | Captures stakeholder needs as user stories with measurable acceptance criteria. |
| 04 | [Functional Requirements Document (FRD)](04_Functional_Requirements_Document.md) | Details the functional requirements, validations, workflows, notifications, and non-functional expectations. |
| 05 | [Functional Test Cases](05_Functional_Test_Cases.md) | Defines test scenarios to validate requirements, including positive, negative, and exception scenarios. |
| 06 | [Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)](06_Requirements_Traceability_Matrix.md) | Establishes forward and reverse traceability between business requirements, functional requirements, user stories, and test cases. |
| 07 | [User Acceptance Test Plan (UAT)](07_User_Acceptance_Test_Plan.md) | Defines the approach for business-user validation, acceptance criteria, execution, and sign-off. |
| 08 | [Executive Summary](08_Executive_Summary.md) | Provides a concise overview of the project, business value, expected benefits, and financial assessment. |
