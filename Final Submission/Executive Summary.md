### 1. Project Overview

The Project Opportunities & Talent Readiness Enhancement initiative proposes improvements to the existing ThinkPalm Employee Management Portal to help the organization make effective use of available and unallocated employees before considering external recruitment.

The proposed solution provides a structured process for publishing upcoming project opportunities, identifying employee interest, assessing skill suitability and readiness, and supporting informed staffing decisions. It also extends the process to include learning and development activities that help employees address identified skill gaps.

### 2. Business Problem

The current resource identification process may involve manual coordination between Project Managers, Resource Managers, and employees. Employees may have limited visibility into upcoming opportunities, while identifying suitable internal resources can require considerable effort.

This can result in:
- Underutilization of available employee skills.
- Limited visibility into upcoming project requirements.
- Manual effort in identifying and evaluating suitable resources.
- Delays in resource planning and staffing decisions.
- Potential external recruitment when suitable internal resources may be available.

### 3. Proposed Solution

Enhance the existing Employee Management Portal to support the following capabilities:

- **Project Managers:** Create and publish project opportunities with required skills, experience, and timelines.
- **Employees:** View eligible opportunities, compare their recorded skills with requirements, identify skill gaps, express interest, and withdraw interest when required.
- **Resource Managers:** Review employee suitability, readiness, and availability; manage shortlists and waitlists; and make authorized staffing decisions.
- **Learning and Development:** Support learning enrolment, assessments, retries, certification, and authorized skill profile updates.
- **Management:** Access relevant information on talent availability, staffing outcomes, and learning activities through management dashboards.

The system will support resource evaluation and decision-making. Final staffing and allocation decisions will remain with authorized Resource Managers.

### 4. Expected Business Benefits

The proposed enhancement is expected to deliver the following benefits:

- Improve utilization of existing and unallocated resources.
- Increase visibility of project opportunities and employee skills.
- Reduce manual effort involved in resource identification and coordination.
- Identify skill gaps and support targeted employee development.
- Improve resource planning and staffing decisions.
- Reduce avoidable external recruitment by reviewing suitable internal resources first.

### 5. Scope Summary

**In Scope**
- Project opportunity creation, publishing, updating, and closure.
- Employee opportunity discovery and eligibility validation.
- Skill comparison and gap identification.
- Expression and withdrawal of interest.
- Resource suitability and readiness assessment.
- Shortlist, waitlist, and staffing outcome management.
- Learning, assessment, retry, and certification support.
- Notifications, role-based access, and management dashboards.
- Relevant data migration and integration considerations.

**Out of Scope**
- Automatic employee allocation to projects.
- Replacement of the organization's complete recruitment process.
- Automatic approval of employee skill updates without authorization.
- Redesign of unrelated Employee Management Portal modules.

### 6. Key Business Rules and Controls

The solution must enforce the following important business controls:

- Employees serving a notice period are not eligible to express interest in internal project opportunities.
- Only employees meeting approved eligibility and availability conditions can participate.
- Employee interest does not guarantee selection or project allocation.
- Final staffing decisions remain with authorized Resource Managers.
- Internal resource availability must be reviewed before external recruitment is considered, in accordance with the approved process.
- Personal and employee information must be accessible only to authorized users for legitimate business purposes.

### 7. Financial Assessment

The Business Case includes an illustrative financial scenario to demonstrate the potential value of reducing avoidable external recruitment and staffing coordination effort.

The scenario estimates a potential annual benefit of **₹5,25,000**, based on assumed recruitment cost avoidance and coordination effort savings. These figures are illustrative and are not confirmed ThinkPalm financial data.

Actual investment costs, operational baselines, measurable savings, ROI, and payback period must be validated with the relevant stakeholders before an investment decision is made.

### 8. Success Measures

The effectiveness of the proposed solution can be evaluated using the following measures:

- Percentage of published opportunities receiving employee interest.
- Number of available employees considered for internal opportunities.
- Percentage of opportunities filled through internal resources.
- Reduction in resource identification and coordination effort.
- Number of employees completing learning activities to address skill gaps.
- Reduction in avoidable external recruitment.

Baseline values and target thresholds must be agreed with stakeholders.

### 9. Conclusion

The Project Opportunities & Talent Readiness Enhancement initiative aims to improve how ThinkPalm identifies, evaluates, and utilizes its existing workforce. By connecting project opportunities, employee skills, resource assessment, and learning activities through the existing portal, the proposed solution can support better workforce utilization and more informed staffing decisions.

The initiative is recommended for stakeholder review and validation of business requirements, financial assumptions, operational dependencies, and implementation feasibility before proceeding to approval.
