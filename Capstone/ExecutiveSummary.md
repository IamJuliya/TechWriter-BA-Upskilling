
# Executive Summary

## Project Opportunities & Talent Readiness Enhancement

**Organization:** ThinkPalm (case-study scenario)  
**Prepared by:** Your Name  
**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Date:** 13 October 2026

### Business Challenge

Employees who are available or unallocated may not always have visibility into upcoming project opportunities or the skills needed for those projects. Resource Managers may need to collect and compare employee skills, readiness, and availability before identifying suitable internal resources.

### Proposed Solution

Enhance the existing Employee Management Portal to provide a centralized project-opportunity and talent-readiness process.

Project Managers publish upcoming opportunities and required skills. Employees review opportunities, compare their recorded skills, identify gaps, and express interest. Resource Managers assess suitability, readiness, and availability before making staffing decisions.

L&D supports employees who need additional skills through tailored learning, assessment, and certification. Management can review talent availability, staffing outcomes, and training information.

### Expected Business Benefits

- Better visibility into upcoming project opportunities.
- More structured comparison of project requirements and employee skills.
- Earlier identification of skill gaps and learning needs.
- Better-informed internal staffing decisions.
- Improved coordination between Project Managers, Resource Managers, employees, and L&D.
- Support for considering suitable internal resources before external recruitment.
- Better visibility into training outcomes and talent readiness.

### Financial Perspective

The enhancement may help improve utilization of existing employees and reduce avoidable external recruitment. However, financial savings are not guaranteed.

The organization should establish a baseline and measure internal staffing outcomes, recruitment costs, training costs, and relevant time-to-staff metrics using approved data and definitions.

### Key Deliverables

1. Business Requirements Document (BRD).
2. Functional Requirements Document (FRD).
3. AS-IS and TO-BE process flow diagrams.
4. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Test Plan.
5. Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM).
6. Stakeholder review and sign-off template.

### Proposed Success Measures

- At least 80% of eligible published opportunities receive interest within 14 days.
- At least 90% of initial Resource Manager reviews are completed within five business days of the agreed review point.
- At least 95% of published opportunities meet defined mandatory data-quality checks.
- Establish and track baseline internal staffing utilization, learning outcomes, and financial impact.

These are proposed targets that require stakeholder validation.

### Key Risks

- Incomplete or outdated employee skill information.
- Project requirements changing after employees express interest.
- Delays in Resource Manager review or L&D activities.
- Unclear readiness and assessment rules.
- Financial benefits being overstated without reliable baseline data.

### Recommendation

Proceed with stakeholder validation and implementation planning. Maintain human control over staffing decisions, protect employee information through role-based access, and measure results using agreed business and financial definitions.

**Case-study note:** The project assumptions and success targets are proposed for this capstone and are not verified ThinkPalm operational data.
