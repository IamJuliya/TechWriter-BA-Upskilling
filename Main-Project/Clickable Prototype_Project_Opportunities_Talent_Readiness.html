End-to-end process
5 roles
1
PM publishes demand
Skills, start date, duration and project context.
2
Employee explores and responds
Compare recorded skills, identify gaps and express or withdraw interest.
3
L&D supports readiness
Learning, assessment result and follow-up are recorded.
4
RM reviews and decides
Consider fit, evidence, availability and timing.
5
Management monitors outcomes
Internal talent use, demand coverage and development trends.
Business value: improve visibility of unallocated resources and existing skills, support internal project matching, identify targeted upskilling, and help optimize staffing budgets. Any cost avoidance shown in this demo is an illustrative estimate, not a confirmed saving.
