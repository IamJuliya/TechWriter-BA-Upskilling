# Stakeholder Review and Sign-Off Document

**Project Title:** Project Opportunities & Talent Readiness Enhancement  
**Organization:** ThinkPalm (Case Study)

**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Document Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to record the simulated stakeholder review of the proposed enhancement to the ThinkPalm Employee Management Portal. It captures the key review areas, expected stakeholder feedback, outstanding business decisions, and proposed approval status.

This document is prepared as part of a Business Analyst upskilling project. No actual review or approval has been obtained from ThinkPalm or its employees.

## 2. Documents Reviewed

The following project deliverables are included in the simulated review:

1. Business Requirements Document (BRD)
2. Functional Requirements Document (FRD)
3. AS-IS and TO-BE Process Flow Diagrams
4. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Test Plan
5. Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
6. Executive Summary
7. Solution Architecture Diagram

## 3. Stakeholders and Review Responsibilities

| Stakeholder | Review Focus |
|---|---|
| Project Manager (PM) | Project opportunity creation, required skills, and opportunity publication |
| Resource Manager (RM) | Employee skill matching, readiness, availability, and final staffing decisions |
| Employee Representative | Opportunity visibility, skill-gap identification, and expressing or withdrawing interest |
| Learning & Development (L&D) Representative | Learning recommendations, assessments, and certification support |
| Management Representative | Resource utilization, staffing effectiveness, reporting, and potential recruitment-cost optimization |

*Note: These are representative stakeholder roles defined for the case study, not confirmed ThinkPalm participants.*

## 4. Simulated Review Outcomes

| Review Area | Simulated Outcome |
|---|---|
| Business objectives and scope | Aligned with the objective of improving utilization of available employees |
| Project opportunity management | Requirements cover creating, validating, and publishing project opportunities |
| Employee participation | Requirements cover viewing opportunities, expressing interest, and withdrawing interest where permitted |
| Skill and readiness evaluation | RM reviews recorded skills, readiness, and availability before making staffing decisions |
| Learning support | L&D supports skill development and assessments where required |
| Notifications and reporting | Requirements identify the need for relevant notifications and management visibility |
| UAT and traceability | Test scenarios and traceability are planned to validate requirements and business outcomes |
| Financial benefits | Improved utilization may reduce avoidable external recruitment costs; actual savings require baseline data and measurement |

**Overall simulated outcome:** The proposed solution is suitable for continued design and validation, subject to resolving the outstanding business decisions listed below. This is a hypothetical review outcome, not an actual stakeholder decision.

## 5. Open Decisions and Action Items

The following items require confirmation from the relevant business stakeholders before implementation:

| ID | Open Decision / Action | Responsible Role |
|---|---|---|
| SD-01 | Confirm the source of employee skill data and the process for keeping it current. | RM / HR |
| SD-02 | Define measurable criteria for employee readiness and skill-match evaluation. | RM / PM |
| SD-03 | Confirm who can create, edit, publish, and close project opportunities. | PM / Management |
| SD-04 | Define notification triggers, recipients, and communication channels. | Business Owner / IT |
| SD-05 | Confirm access permissions and visibility rules for employee skill and readiness information. | HR / IT |
| SD-06 | Establish baseline measures and targets for resource utilization, staffing turnaround time, and recruitment-cost impact. | Management / Finance |
| SD-07 | Confirm the process to follow when no suitable internal employee is available. | RM / Management |
| SD-08 | Validate the proposed requirements and UAT acceptance criteria with actual business stakeholders. | Business Owner / QA |

Recommended next steps:
